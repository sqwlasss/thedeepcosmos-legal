Privacy Policy

Astro Studio · Effective date: 2026-11-14

Astro Studio is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @thedeepcosmos. This page explains what data it touches and what happens to it.

1. What data is accessed, and why

When the owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit, the app may request these permissions (scopes):

  • user.info.basic: the basic profile information of the authorised account (such as its username and display name), used only to confirm that the signed-in account is @thedeepcosmos.
  • video.upload: to upload the owner's own videos to the account's drafts.
  • video.publish: to post the owner's own videos directly, only after the owner has reviewed and approved each one.

The app also reads the status of the uploads it makes. It does not access any other TikTok data, and it does not collect data about other people, such as viewers, followers or commenters.

2. Where data is stored

OAuth tokens and app credentials are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sent to or kept on a server run by the app. The videos uploaded are the owner's original content and are sent directly to TikTok.

3. Sharing and selling

Data is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or tracking. The app shows no ads. This website uses no cookies, scripts or analytics. (GitHub, which hosts this page, may keep its own standard server logs under its own policies.)

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as they are needed to use the app. The owner can delete them from the computer at any time, and they are deleted on request. Once the tokens are deleted or access is revoked, the app can no longer reach the account.

5. Revoking access

The owner can withdraw the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings, Manage app permissions, by removing Astro Studio. The app will then stop working until the owner authorises it again.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and has no users other than its adult owner. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.

8. Contact

For questions or deletion requests, open an issue on the GitHub Issues page of this repository, or message the TikTok account @thedeepcosmos.

Astro Studio is a personal tool for the TikTok account @thedeepcosmos. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
