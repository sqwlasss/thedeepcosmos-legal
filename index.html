Astro Studio

Effective date: 2026-11-14

Astro Studio is a small personal tool used by one person: the owner of the TikTok account @thedeepcosmos, a space and astronomy channel. It helps the owner prepare short videos and upload them to that same account.

What it does

  • Signs in with TikTok Login Kit, only to the owner's own account.
  • Uploads the owner's own, original videos through TikTok's Content Posting API, either to drafts or as a direct post. The owner reviews and approves every video before it is sent.
  • Checks the status of those uploads.

What it is not

Astro Studio has no other users. It sells nothing, shows no ads, does not collect data about other people, and does not share data with third parties.
